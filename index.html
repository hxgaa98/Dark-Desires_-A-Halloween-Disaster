House Rules

Tap any rule to read more. Everyone’s night depends on them.

🔞
18+ only

Guests must be 18 or older. Bring a valid photo ID (MyKad, driving licence or passport). No ID, no entry, and no refund.

💌
Closed private event, by invitation

Entry is for invited guests who have RSVP’d and paid. Plus-ones must also RSVP. Walk-ins will not be admitted.

🎭
Costumes encouraged

Dress to impress, but keep it tasteful. Masks are welcome, though you may be asked to lift yours at the door for ID checks.

🚫
STRICTLY NO DRUGS

No illegal drugs or substances of any kind, whether consumed, carried or shared. Offenders will be removed immediately and may be reported to the authorities.

🗡️
No weapons, real or realistic

Costume props must be clearly fake and harmless. Anything that looks like a real weapon will be held at the door.

🔥
No open flames or fireworks

Please leave lighters, sparklers and fire props outside. Candles are handled by the host.

🍾
Outside drinks are allowed

Bring your own favourites to share or enjoy. Please keep them in sealed or original containers. The bar and cocktails are provided as well.

🔒
Know your limits

Drink responsibly. Never leave your drink unattended, and never accept a drink you did not see being poured.

🚖
Do not drink and drive

Arrange a Grab or e-hailing ride, or a designated driver. Hosts may refuse entry or ask guests to stop drinking if they’re unsafe.

🤝
Consent is everything

Flirt freely, but only with a yes. Harassment, unwanted touching or pressure leads to immediate removal.

📵
Ask before you photograph

Do not photograph or film guests without their permission, and do not post anyone who asks you not to.

🛡️
Right of admission reserved

The hosts may refuse entry or ask any guest to leave to keep the event safe and fun for everyone.
